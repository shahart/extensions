# Privacy Policy for URL Volume Limiter

Last updated: 2026-11-15

URL Volume Limiter does not collect or transmit personal data, browsing history, or website content. It does not use analytics, cookies, or third-party services.

URL prefixes that you add and the maximum volume setting are stored locally in Microsoft Edge so the extension can apply your chosen volume cap. The extension also checks media source addresses on matching pages. These values are not synced, recorded, or sent to a server.

Remove a URL from the extension popup to delete it from the extension's local settings. You can also revoke site access from Microsoft Edge's extension settings.

This policy may be updated if the extension's data practices change. For privacy questions, contact the publisher through the support details listed on the Microsoft Edge Add-ons listing.
